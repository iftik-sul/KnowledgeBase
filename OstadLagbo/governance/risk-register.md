# Risk Register

Risks are scored Likelihood × Impact (Low / Medium / High). Owner is the founder for all entries while the team is one person; the column exists so ownership transfers cleanly later. Reviewed at each planning milestone and before each launch gate. **Reviews: 2026-08-30 (end of requirements + policy phase) · 2026-09-14 (end of technical design: data models, ADR-001).**

| ID | Risk | L | I | Mitigation | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | **Supply-side cold start** — an empty map gives Shagreds nothing to find, killing the marketplace on arrival | H | H | Seed before demand: hand-recruit 30–50 Ostads in one launch area with white-glove onboarding, starting at **Slice 1** (OL-BLD-001) in the three launch-focus categories (OL-SKC-001); bilingual UI (CL-016) and Bangla search (CL-013) lower onboarding friction. **The discovery sprint was declined on 2026-09-12; the supply-side assumptions (six-stage onboarding, NID upload, live selfie holding ID) are unvalidated by decision** — Slice 1's first real recruits are the first validation | Open — unvalidated by decision |
| R-02 | **Identity-data exposure** — NID/passport/licence images and selfies are high-value breach targets | M | H | Encrypted storage; admin-only access with audit logging (ADM-17); Retention & Deletion Policy in force (OL-RET-001); identity vault modeled with per-row purge and 12-month number retention (OL-REG-DM-001); managed encryption at rest via ADR-001's data tier; retention tooling (ADM-18) to be verified in staging before launch | Partially mitigated — policy and model done, tooling pending build |
| R-03 | **Trust & safety incident** — the platform leads strangers to meet in person | M | H | 18+ accounts; identity verification + approval before discoverability; report, block, suspension, and **termination** (CL-019) in MVP; chat retained as moderation evidence; Incident Response process defined (OL-INC-001) with safety playbook and standing pre-launch preparations | Partially mitigated — process defined, prep checklist open |
| R-04 | **Exact-pin exposure** — public exact pins can point at Ostads' homes | M | H | Baseline rider: location capture states the pin is public and encourages pinning a coaching center, shop, or landmark (MAP-01); ToS §4 makes pin-placement responsibility explicit; viewer coordinates never persisted or logged (OL-MAP-DM-001) | Open |
| R-05 | **Solo-founder overload** — one person is sponsor, PM, builder, and admin reviewer | H | H | Ruthless scope enforced by baseline change control (20 changes logged, none silent); the build sequence's usable-at-every-gate staircase (OL-BLD-001); documentation kept hire-ready; admin workflow documented for transfer; support tickets structure the inbound load | Open |
| R-06 | **Build weight vs. capacity** — the committed build includes chat with voice, push, portfolio media, a full admin panel with charts, support tickets, insights, termination, and bilingual UI | H | H | **ADR-001 (2026-09-13) chose a managed three-tier stack** — Supabase for data services, Render for a conventional API, Vercel for web — selected specifically so the AI builder writes conventional, testable code and no infrastructure is self-operated; slices sized in the schedule phase; any further scope addition names its cost. Likelihood stays High until the schedule sizes the slices honestly | Open — mitigation concrete, sizing pending |
| R-07 | **App-store rejection** — user-generated content, chat, and identity collection attract Google Play / App Store scrutiny | M | H | Privacy Policy and ToS drafted (OL-PRV-001, OL-TOS-001); report/block/moderation specified; consent capture (REG-13); guest-reachable legal docs (MAP-03, public website CL-020); 18+ gate; compliance checklist remains before submission | Partially mitigated — documents drafted, legal review pending |
| R-08 | **SMS/OTP cost and abuse** — OTP flows are a spend and spam vector | M | M | Password login keeps OTP to signup/reset/phone-change; rate limits (REG-02); SMS monitor (ADM-19); **the Bangladesh SMS gateway is ADR-001's largest variable cost and an open procurement item for Slice 0** | Open |
| R-09 | **Fake or low-quality profiles and reviews** — reputation fraud destroys the trust promise | L | M | Mitigated by design: admin approves every profile; identity gate (ADM-03) with duplicate-ID blocking (CL-012) against current documents and banned tombstones; accepted-offer-only reviews, one per pair, removal consumes the slot (OL-RNT-DM-001); monitor at soft launch | Mitigated by design |
| R-10 | **Bangladesh data-protection obligations** — the Personal Data Protection Act, 2026 governs identity and location data, with penalties up to 5% of turnover | M | M | PDPA mapped: retention durations disclosed (OL-RET-001/PRV-001), consent capture with versioned records (REG-13), erasure via self-service deletion, breach notification (OL-INC-001), consent records retained 3 years. **Now specific: identity documents will be stored in Singapore (ADR-001) — the cross-border-transfer question is ADR-001 open item 1 and must be ruled on before Slice 1 collects the first NID; the fallback is an encrypted in-country backup replica.** The Privacy Policy needs a v1.1 refresh to disclose the transfer before legal review | Partially mitigated — mapped and drafted; residency ruling and policy refresh pending |
| R-11 | **AI-generated code defects and security flaws** — the builder is an AI agent; subtly wrong logic or an insecure default can ship without a human engineer noticing | M | H | ADR-001's conventional, testable stack chosen for this reason; NFR-14 requires automated tests covering every acceptance criterion of Slices 0–3 before soft launch; Supabase row-level security as a second line of defense behind the API policy layer (ADR-001); dependency vulnerability scanning in CI (NFR-05); adversarial review of generated code at every slice gate, applying the same discipline the data-model reviews used; the knowledge base itself is the specification the agent is held to, and "gaps are raised, never invented" (project standards) | Open — added 2026-09-14 |

## Review notes

**2026-09-14:** R-11 added — the risk of the builder itself was unrecorded. R-01 now states plainly that validation was declined by decision, so no future reader mistakes the assumptions for tested. R-06's mitigation became concrete with ADR-001 but its likelihood is deliberately left High until the schedule sizes each slice. R-10 moved from abstract to specific: Singapore. R-01 and R-05 remain the two existential risks; R-11 is the new third. Next scheduled review: at schedule/budget completion, then before soft launch.

**2026-08-30:** R-06's likelihood was raised to High — the founder chose a full-featured MVP with eyes open. R-04 stands because of a deliberate founder decision (exact pins, baseline §3); if incident reports at soft launch involve location exposure, the mitigation escalates to a baseline change proposal.
