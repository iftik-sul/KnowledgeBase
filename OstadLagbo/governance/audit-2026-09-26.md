# Whole-KB Audit — 2026-09-26

Full read of all 62 `OstadLagbo/` documents (~80,000 words) at the start of execution, checking for contradictions, stale content, gaps, and mistakes. Focus: places where ADR-003/ADR-004 (accepted 2026-09-25) were not carried back into the older layers, plus product-logic seams and launch-readiness holes an AI build agent or a real launch would hit.

**Overall:** traceability is essentially complete — every requirement across the eight modules traces to a data model, an API endpoint, and a UI screen, and the privacy invariants (no stored Shagred coordinates, the opacity rule, counts-only insights) hold across all four layers. The defects cluster in three places: (1) the two newest ADRs contradict the documents they should have superseded, (2) a handful of product-logic rules disagree between layers, and (3) operational/launch items are named but not specified. Nothing here is a rewrite — it is one alignment pass plus a short list of founder decisions.

Severity: **High** = would cause a wrong build, a legal exposure, or a launch blocker · **Medium** = confusion/rework · **Low** = hygiene.

## 2. Product-logic contradictions between layers
