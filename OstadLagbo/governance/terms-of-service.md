# Ostad Lagbo — Terms of Service (Working Draft)

*User-facing document. The agreement between Ostad Lagbo (ওস্তাদ লাগবো) and everyone who uses it. Plain language by intent. Pending review by a Bangladesh-qualified lawyer before launch; a Bangla version ships alongside the app's Bangla release.*

## 1. What Ostad Lagbo is — and is not

Ostad Lagbo is a **discovery and connection platform**: it helps learners (Shagreds) find local teachers and experts (Ostads), evaluate their profiles, and make contact. That is where our role ends. **We are not a party to any teaching arrangement.** Ostads are independent people, not our employees, agents, or contractors. We do not set, collect, or guarantee prices; we do not schedule, host, supervise, or certify lessons; we do not guarantee that any offer will be accepted, that any lesson will occur, or the quality or outcome of any teaching. Whatever you and the other person agree — location, timing, price, everything — is between the two of you, at your own judgment and responsibility.

## 2. Verification is not endorsement

The verified badge means one thing: the Ostad submitted a government identity document and passed our review process. It does **not** mean we vouch for their skill, character, or safety, and it is not a background check. Reviews reflect other users' opinions, not ours. You remain responsible for your own judgment — especially when meeting anyone in person. Meet first in public places, involve family where appropriate, and trust your instincts.

## 3. Who may use it

You must be **18 or older** to hold an account; we enforce this by date of birth at registration. Guardians register in their own name to find teachers for learners under 18 — the account, the communications, and the arrangements belong to the adult guardian. One account per person, one role per account (Ostad or Shagred, permanent), and one account per phone number. You are responsible for keeping your credentials secure and for everything done under your account.

## 4. Your commitments

By using Ostad Lagbo you agree to: provide **truthful information** — legal names matching your identity document, honest professional claims, real portfolio work; use the platform **only for its purpose** — finding or offering skill teaching; and never: impersonate anyone, submit forged documents, harass or threaten anyone, defraud or scam, spam offers, post unlawful or obscene content, write reviews of experiences you did not have, attempt to scrape our data, or circumvent a suspension or block. Ostads specifically agree that the map pin they place is publicly visible at exact precision and choose its location accordingly.

## 5. Your content

You own what you post — profile content, portfolio, messages, reviews. You grant us a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to store, display, and distribute it **solely to operate the platform** (showing your profile to users, delivering your messages, displaying your reviews). The license ends when the content is deleted per our retention policy, except content we must retain for legal or safety reasons.

## 6. Offers, chat, and phone numbers

Offers expire after 7 days if unanswered; you may withdraw a pending offer; a declined offer may be re-sent, and repeated unwanted contact is grounds for blocking and enforcement. **Accepting an offer reveals both parties' phone numbers to each other — this cannot be undone.** Chats support text and voice notes, are private between participants, and may be reviewed by our moderation team only when reported, with access logged.

## 7. Moderation and enforcement

To keep the marketplace trustworthy we may, with recorded reasons: request changes to or reject profile submissions, remove reviews or replies that violate these terms, warn accounts, suspend accounts (which freezes chats and removes map presence), and ban repeat or severe violators — including retaining minimal identity data to prevent banned users from returning, as described in the Privacy Policy. Reports are reviewed by a human. If you believe an enforcement action was wrong, contact us (Section 12) and we will review it.
