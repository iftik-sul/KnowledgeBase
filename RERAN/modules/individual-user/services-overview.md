# Individual User Services Overview

## Business Services

### 1. Verification Services

These services allow users to verify the legitimacy of real estate participants, development projects, and properties before engaging in a transaction.

* Service \#1 – Verify Developer  
* Service \#2 – Verify Development Project  
* Service \#3 – Verify Property

### 2. Property Ownership & Transaction Services

These services enable users to register, transfer, update, and manage property ownership and property-related transactions in accordance with RERAN regulations.

* Service \#4 – Register Property Ownership  
* Service \#5 – Transfer Property Ownership  
* Service \#6 – Register Property Sale  
* Service \#7 – Update Property Ownership Information  
* Service \#8 – Register Sale of Mortgaged Property  
* Service \#9 – Register Gift Transfer  
* Service \#10 – Register Lease-to-Own  
* Service \#11 – Transfer Lease-to-Own  
* Service \#12 – Release Lease-to-Own  
* Service \#13 – Amend Lease-to-Own  
* Service \#14 – Register Usufruct Right  
* Service \#15 – Amend Usufruct Right  
* Service \#16 – Terminate Usufruct Right

### 3. Title & Land Registration Services

These services allow users to register, update, and manage land ownership, title records, and other statutory land registration activities regulated by RERAN.

* Service \#17 – Grant Registration  
* Service \#18 – Grant Completion  
* Service \#19 – Register Heirs Ownership  
* Service \#20 – Register Community Land  
* Service \#21 – Register Partners Division  
* Service \#22 – Register Industrial & Commercial Land Ownership

### 4. Tenancy Services

These services support tenancy registration, lease renewal, tenancy management, valuation, and dispute resolution.

* Service \#23 – Register Lease  
* Service \#24 – Renew Lease  
* Service \#25 – Manage Lease  
* Service \#26 – Submit Tenancy Dispute  
* Service \#27 – Cancel Tenancy Contract  
* Service \#28 – Request Rental Valuation

### 5. Power of Attorney Services

These services enable property owners to legally authorize another individual to act on their behalf for property-related matters.

* Service \#29 – Register Power of Attorney  
* Service \#30 – Act on Behalf of Property Owner

### 6. Property Information & Certificate Services

These services allow users to request official property records, certificates, surveys, and valuation documents maintained by RERAN.

* Service \#31 – Request Detailed Real Estate Statement  
* Service \#32 – Request To Whom It May Concern Certificate  
* Service \#33 – Request Property Survey  
* Service \#34 – Request Property Valuation  
* Service \#35 – Request Full / Partial Indemnity

### 7. Diaspora Services

These services enable Nigerians living abroad to securely access RERAN services remotely.

* Service \#36 – Remote Identity Verification  
* Service \#37 – Remote Property Transactions

### 8. Consumer Protection Services

These services provide mechanisms for users to lodge complaints and monitor complaint resolution.

* Service \#38 – Submit Complaint  
* Service \#39 – Track Complaint

## The Difference

### Business Services

Business Services represent the regulatory services that RERAN provides to Individual Users. Each service has its own business process, eligibility requirements, required documents, service fees, approval workflow, and regulatory outcome.

**Examples:**

* Service \#4 – Register Property Ownership  
* Service \#23 – Register Lease  
* Service \#38 – Submit Complaint

### Shared Platform Features

Shared Platform Features are reusable platform capabilities that support one or more Business Services. They do not represent standalone regulatory services but provide common functionality across the platform.

**Examples:**

* Submit Application  
* Track Application Status  
* Payments  
* Documents  
* Notifications  
* Profile & KYC
