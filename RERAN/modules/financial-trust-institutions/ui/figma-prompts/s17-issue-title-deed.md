**Module:** Financial & Trust Institutions (RERAN Group C)

**How to use:** each prompt below is self-contained. Copy one block at a time into Figma AI.

## Assumptions baked into these prompts

| # | Assumption | Why |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1 | Designed as an **institution-portal** flow (FI sidebar, logged-in institution user) | Source only documents a Land Department walk-in path; a bank-originated channel is an open question. Portal shell chosen for consistency with every other screen in this file. |
| 2 | **Payment is a step inside the wizard** | Service #17 pays *before* RERA review. The "services #12–#18 skip payment" rule in the spec doesn't hold for this service's actual sequence. |
| 3 | Tracker uses the **built** 6-step labels | Matches your existing Application Review screen, not the spec doc. |
| 4 | **Validation has no standalone screen** | It auto-passes between Documents and Payment; it stays as a tracker step and surfaces as the Validation Summary card on Review & Submit. |
| 5 | Fee figures are **placeholders** | Real fee is unresolved client data. Shape matches your sample: Service Fee + Processing Levy + 7.5% VAT. |
