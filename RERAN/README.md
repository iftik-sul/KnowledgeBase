# RERAN

## Purpose

RERAN is a digital real estate regulatory platform designed to manage the registration, regulation, and lifecycle of real estate stakeholders, properties, development projects, transactions, and regulatory services. It provides a unified system for citizens, businesses, developers, financial institutions, agents, and regulatory authorities to interact through standardized digital workflows.
